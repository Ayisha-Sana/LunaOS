LunaOS — Lunar Habitat Operating System

*Think smart. Survive lunar.*


**LunaOS** is an interactive lunar habitat simulation where you take command of a Moon outpost and make the engineering decisions needed to keep its crew alive for 30 simulated days.

Manage life support, energy, food production, radiation protection, and system maintenance. Respond to unexpected events, balance limited resources, and discover how decisions in one subsystem can affect an entire mission.

The objective isn't simply to survive. It's to understand what survival actually takes.

✨ Features

- *Mission Control:* Monitor crew status, resource reserves, habitat conditions, and mission progress.
- *Resource Management:* Balance oxygen, water, food, and battery energy.
- *Systems & Engineering:* Configure life-support recycling, greenhouse production, scientific activity, and maintenance.
- *Upgrades & Repairs:* Invest mission credits in improving habitat systems and resolving faults.
- *Dynamic Events:* Respond to unexpected events that introduce new challenges and decisions.
- *Mission Log:* Track events, decisions, warnings, and daily developments.
- *Mission Analysis:* Review mission performance, resource trends, system issues, and important decisions.
- *Interactive Forecasts:* Explore projected energy reserves and resource trends.

🛰️ How to Play

1. Initialize your mission: Choose your crew size and difficulty level.
2. Monitor your habitat: Check available resources, system conditions, and warnings.
3. Configure your systems: Adjust recycling, agriculture, research, and maintenance settings.
4. Make decisions: Respond to mission events, purchase upgrades, and repair faults.
5. Advance each day: Observe how your decisions affect the habitat.
6. Complete the mission: Survive all 30 simulated days and review your mission debrief.

Every decision involves a trade-off. More greenhouse production, for example, can improve future food availability while increasing energy demand.

🔬 Scientific Context

LunaOS explores engineering trade-offs associated with sustaining a crewed lunar habitat, including life support, energy availability, resource recycling, food production, and radiation protection.

The simulation uses simplified numerical models and game assumptions to make these concepts interactive and accessible. Its resource values and outcomes should not be interpreted as validated engineering specifications for a real lunar mission.

🎯 Project Inspiration

LunaOS was developed around the idea that students understand engineering more deeply when they can experiment with decisions and experience their consequences.

Instead of simply reading about the challenges of living on the Moon, players get to manage a habitat, make difficult choices, and learn from the results.

*A lunar mission is more than reaching the Moon. It's figuring out how to stay.*
